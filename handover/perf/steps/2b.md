## Decisions for the user

1. `wholeLines` 500,000 (decision says ~1M), `lineWidth` 20,000 (lines are cut where syntax turns off; Open in Editor for the rest), preview 10,000 lines / 1 MB, `analysisInFlight` 8 MB, analysis budget 10 s and 2M captures per query.

3. `Limit.patch` 256 MB: a review whose patch is larger still fails with git's message (no fallback).
4. Notice wording (in `admissionNotices` and `Admission.reason`).
